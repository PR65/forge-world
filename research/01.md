# Research Note: IndiaBUILD Public-Site Extraction & Conceptual Framework

## Scope

This research note provides a comprehensive structural, architectural, and operational extraction of **IndiaBUILD** (`indiabuild.wiki`), a living open-access intelligence wiki and hard-technology mapping project launched by *Swarajya* (Kovai Media Private Limited) in 2026. The scope encompasses all public sections of the platform, including its sector taxonomies (~22 hard-technology areas spanning aerospace, semiconductors, defence, power and energy, heavy engineering, etc.), capability hierarchy and dependency "tech trees", 6-tier maturity level taxonomy, builder registry, policy and mission overlays, investor relationship mapping, thematic collections, the weekly changes feed, the automated 5-step intelligence engine methodology, source verification discipline, API/export availability, licensing terms, and direct conceptual utility for physical-industry planning systems (Forge World).

---

## Executive Findings

1. **Scale and Mapping Footprint**: As of August 2026, IndiaBUILD tracks **7,047 total capabilities** (with **3,874 capabilities assessed** and **3,173 unassessed**), **1,835+ builders**, **1,544 still-red gaps**, **96 government policies/missions**, **101 investors**, and over **450 researched pages**. A striking macro finding highlighted by the platform is that **70% of assessed hard-technology capabilities remain red (no capability) or amber (emerging)**, underscoring deep structural dependency chokepoints in India's industrial ecosystem.
2. **Four Core Entity Architecture**: The platform operates on four interconnected entities that cross-link automatically:
   - **Capabilities**: The spine of the map, covering finished products (e.g., narrowbody jets, port cranes) and foundational technology nodes (e.g., single crystal blade casting, EDA software, polysilicon).
   - **Builders**: Companies, startups, academic labs, and government programmes attempting specific capabilities, tracked with dual status (self-reported builder claims versus independent evidence-based assessments).
   - **Policies**: Central government schemes, missions, tariff measures, standards, and export controls (e.g., Semicon India Programme at ₹76,000 crore, National Critical Minerals Mission at ₹34,300 crore) mapped directly to targeted capabilities and beneficiary builders.
   - **Investors**: Venture capital, growth equity, sovereign, and government funds financing builders. Notably, IndiaBUILD enforces a strict rule: **it records who funds whom, but never deal sizes, round amounts, or fund totals**.
3. **Maturity Hierarchy (6 Tiers)**: IndiaBUILD standardizes industrial capability maturity across six distinct, color-coded levels:
   - *No capability* (imported or absent; red)
   - *Emerging* (R&D or government programme exists, nothing fielded; amber)
   - *Demonstrated* (working Indian prototype/version exists, but not at scale or commercial quality; yellow)
   - *Producing (licensed)* (manufactured in India at scale, but on a foreign design — not sovereign; light blue)
   - *Producing (own tech)* (manufactured at scale on India's own design; dark blue)
   - *Competitive* (world-class in both scale and quality; green)
4. **The Intelligence Engine Pipeline**: Platform content is dynamically maintained by an automated 5-step background queue engine running every five minutes:
   - *Research*: Live web search investigating primary and authoritative sources.
   - *Verify*: Independent model re-fetching cited sources with zero memory of the research step to validate support (claims with failing sources are dropped; disagreements are marked *contested*).
   - *Reconcile*: Semantic deduplication across capabilities, builders, policies, and investors to prevent duplicate records, biasing toward "new when unsure" while leaving automatic merging strictly prohibited (human operator review required).
   - *Write*: Closed-book essay generation operating exclusively on verified facts (no web access during writing).
   - *Record*: Transactional commit of articles, versioned history, dependency edges, and stubs.
   - *Quality Gates*: Indexing in search engines requires at least 85% citation survival; the *human-verified* tier is awarded exclusively by human operators.
5. **API & Data Export Status**: As of August 2026, IndiaBUILD provides **no dedicated public developer REST API or bulk JSON/CSV download export endpoint**. Data ingestion occurs entirely through the public web interface (`indiabuild.wiki`) and the internal background queue engine.
6. **Licensing & Terms**: Operated under *Swarajya* (Kovai Media Private Limited); public browsing is entirely free, with contributions channeled through a public suggestion ledger. Commercial tiers (such as *Swarajya PRO*) bundle direct contributions to domestic builder funds.

## Relationships and Variables Useful for Forge World

For a physical-industry planning system like **Forge World**, IndiaBUILD offers an advanced blueprint for mapping hard-technology dependencies and industrial chokepoints:
1. **Directed Tech Trees (Dependencies vs. Unlocks)**: The formal separation between finished products (e.g., passenger jets, power blocks) and foundational nodes (e.g., turbine superalloys, EDA software, vacuum interrupters) enables precise downstream impact modeling. Closing a single foundational gap unlocks multiple dependent product trees.
2. **Dual-Status Verification Framework**: Maintaining parallel tracks for *builder self-reported claims* versus *independent evidence-based assessments* prevents over-optimistic industrial planning and filters out marketing noise.
3. **Capital-Starved Gap Identification**: Automatically flagging capabilities that have active builder interest but **zero investor backing** (e.g., carbon-fibre composite manufacturing for aero structures, epoxy resin casting) provides an immediate priority matrix for state intervention, venture allocation, or public-private funding.
4. **Policy-to-Capability-to-Builder Triangulation**: Linking government missions (tariffs, PLI schemes, RDI grants) directly to specific technology chokepoints and beneficiary enterprises creates an auditable policy-efficacy matrix.
5. **Bounded Intelligence Engine Architecture**: The separation of research, independent verification, and closed-book writing provides a robust architectural template for autonomous agent systems maintaining factual rigor in technical domains.

## Gaps and Unknowns

1. **Absence of Programmatic Export Interfaces**: The lack of public APIs, GraphQL endpoints, or bulk JSON data dumps requires reliance on web scraping or manual navigation, hindering automated synchronization for external planning engines.
2. **High Ratio of Unassessed Nodes**: With 3,173 out of 7,047 capabilities currently unassessed (and over 45% lacking deep verification), wide swaths of secondary and tertiary sectors remain stub entries.
3. **Granularity of Cost and Timeline Variables**: While the engine tracks qualitative maturity stages and relationship links, it deliberately excludes quantitative cost estimates, capital round sizes, and exact production ramp timelines, limiting bottom-up economic modeling.
