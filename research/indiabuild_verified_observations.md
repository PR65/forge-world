# IndiaBUILD verified observations (browser, 22 Aug 2026)

## Homepage: https://indiabuild.wiki/

The public homepage describes IndiaBUILD as a living map of hard-technology capabilities and shows a dated changes feed. The page displayed an update date of Aug 21, 2026 and the following live dashboard metrics: 7,047 capabilities mapped; 1,544 still-red gaps; 1,835 builders in the arena; 96 schemes; and 101 investors. It also displayed 3,874 assessed capabilities and 3,173 not yet assessed, with maturity counts: No capability 471; Emerging 1,073; Demonstrated 681; Producing (licensed) 468; Producing (own tech) 1,067; Competitive 114.

The site defines six visible maturity states: No capability, Emerging, Demonstrated, Producing (licensed), Producing (own tech), and Competitive. The homepage presents capabilities as linked to builders, blockers, schemes/investors, and dependency relationships. It also labels a "naked gap" as a red capability with zero builders and a "capital-starved gap" as a capability with builders but zero investors.

Examples visible on the page include aerospace single-crystal blade casting as the spotlight, with PTC Industries/Aerolloy Technologies, DMRL/DRDO, and IISc Bangalore listed as builders; hard-carbon anode production and battery-cell assembly equipment as recent status moves; and semiconductor equipment metrology/inspection, advanced process design kit and EDA toolchain, thin-film deposition, and power semiconductor valves among the leading chokepoints.

The homepage states that suggestions are researched, verified against sources, drafted, and human-approved; it also advertises a public ledger for suggestions and outcomes. It describes a catalogue of 10,000 hard capabilities, but the live dashboard showed 7,047 mapped capabilities at the time observed; this distinction should be preserved rather than treated as a contradiction.

## Engine page: https://indiabuild.wiki/engine

The page explicitly describes a five-step pipeline: Research, Verify, Reconcile, Write, and Record. Research uses live web search and primary/authoritative sources. Verify is performed by a second independent model with fresh context and no memory of the research; it re-fetches cited sources and asks whether the page actually supports each claim. Claims failing verification are dropped, and disagreements are retained as contested. The page says rejected facts do not reach writing.

The engine page also shows the site’s conceptual relevance to FORGE WORLD: source-linked research, independent verification, deduplication/reconciliation, closed-book writing from verified facts, and versioned records. The remainder of the page should be checked for exact reconciliation, writing, recording, quality-gate, and access details before making stronger claims.

## Evidence discipline

These observations are direct website observations and should be cited as FACT with date/time context. They are not independent validation of the underlying dashboard counts or the truth of individual capability assessments. Any claim about API absence, licensing, or internal engine implementation requires separate verification from explicit terms/documentation or cautious wording as "no public endpoint was found during inspection."
