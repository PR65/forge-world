# WIDE RESEARCH MISSION — FORGE WORLD

Conduct an extremely broad, parallel research investigation to build the knowledge foundation for an ambitious future project called **FORGE WORLD**.

Do NOT build the software.

Do NOT design its UI.

Do NOT narrow the research into one company, one sector, Swarajya, IndiaBUILD, robotics, or India.

Those are inputs into a much larger problem.

## THE PROJECT

FORGE WORLD is intended to become a computational system where a user can specify a physical objective such as:

> India manufactures 1,000,000 humanoid robots/year by 2030.

> Europe manufactures 5 million humanoids/year without Chinese critical inputs.

> India manufactures 10,000 satellites/year.

> Africa develops 500 GW/year of solar manufacturing capacity.

> Earth produces 100 reusable launch vehicles/year.

The system should eventually determine:

* what products/components are required;
* what manufacturing processes are required;
* what machine tools and production equipment are required;
* what raw materials/resources are required;
* what factories and infrastructure are required;
* what technology/IP/process know-how is required;
* which countries/companies possess those capabilities;
* what capacity already exists;
* what capacity is missing;
* what must be built;
* how long it takes;
* what it costs where defensible;
* what trade/logistics are required;
* what energy is required;
* which capabilities are chokepoints;
* how industrial shocks propagate;
* what substitutions are possible;
* whether the final target can actually be achieved.

It should eventually work at:

country → multi-country → continent → global scale.

The flagship benchmark is **India producing 1M humanoids/year by 2030**, but the architecture must ultimately support essentially any physical industry.
