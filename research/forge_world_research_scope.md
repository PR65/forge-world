# FORGE WORLD Wide Research Package — Confirmed Scope

## Research streams
1. IndiaBUILD public-site extraction: sectors, capability pages, dependencies, foundational capabilities, blockers, maturity, builders, companies, schemes, investors, collections, history, recent changes, methodology, engine/research methodology, sources, suggestions/ledger, hierarchy, licensing, terms, API/export.
2. Swarajya industrial-intelligence mining: public X corpus and relevant website reporting, limited to physical-economy and industrial-capability signals; preserve claim → source → evidence → confidence.
3. Global capability analogues: WIPO, Harvard Atlas, OECD, UNIDO, national capability databases, defence-industrial mapping, European dependencies, Japanese/Korean/Chinese studies, World Bank, academic datasets, and obscure sources.
4. Physical-economy data: materials/minerals, trade, manufacturing, input-output networks, facilities, energy, logistics, prices, technology/IP, and policy; capture API/bulk, license, coverage, granularity, update frequency, limitations, and Forge usefulness.
5. Industry decomposition sources: BOMs, teardowns, process databases, LCI, customs relationships, engineering datasets, ontologies, knowledge graphs, patents, procurement catalogs, standards, and manuals.
6. Foundational industrial recursion: machine tools, CNC, bearings, spindles, motors, drives, controls, metrology, furnaces, vacuum, lithography, chemical equipment, robotics, tooling, dies, molds, grinding, heat treatment, specialty steels; identify unlocks and dominant suppliers.
7. Supplier qualification and industrial know-how: qualification time, certification, yield, quality systems, learning curves, ramp, recipes, tacit knowledge, IP/licensing, transfer, controls, standards, customer qualification, and manufacturing readiness.
8. Industrial shock case studies: oil shocks, Japan 2011, semiconductor shortage, Suez, Red Sea, rare earths, graphite, COVID, Europe gas, Chinese controls, copper, and container prices; extract event → effects → substitutions → recovery → timing.
9. Technology substitution cases: rare-earth-free motors, battery chemistry, materials, semiconductors, recycling, process substitution, alternate manufacturing, and local redesign; extract bottleneck → alternative → tradeoffs → new dependencies.
10. Existing systems/products: supply-chain digital twins, industrial planning, manufacturing graphs, product-to-supply-chain engines, economic complexity, mineral simulators, strategic supply-chain tools, manufacturing copilots, policy simulation, factory AI, national twins, scenario simulators, geoeconomic models; identify copy/learn/integrate/avoid.
11. India 1M humanoids/year by 2030 benchmark: architecture, components, manufacturing, current global producers, Indian capability, gaps, long-lead bottlenecks, dependencies, energy, qualification, substitutions; ranges only where defensible.
12. Missing-dimensions challenge: industrial planning, manufacturing engineering, economics, supply chain, geology, energy, policy, factory operations, and other consequential omissions.

## Required outputs
- Executive synthesis.
- Broad source map with preserved URLs and source quality labels.
- Dataset/API matrix.
- IndiaBUILD findings and access/licensing notes.
- Swarajya industrial-intelligence findings.
- Existing-system landscape.
- Capability-model, industrial-time, foundational-recursion, shock, substitution, and benchmark dossiers.
- Unexpected findings.
- Recommendations, risks, data gaps, and unknowns.
- Machine-readable appendices (CSV/JSON/Markdown) where feasible.

## Evidence discipline
Every major statement will be labeled FACT, DERIVED INTERPRETATION, MODEL/ESTIMATE, UNCERTAIN CLAIM, or UNKNOWN. Primary sources will be preferred, claims will be cross-checked, and unverified assertions will not be presented as facts.
