# Verified dataset observations (browser, 22 Aug 2026)

## Harvard Atlas of Economic Complexity
URL: https://atlas.hks.harvard.edu/data-downloads/

The Atlas page states that it provides international trade data for over 6,000 products across 250 countries and territories. The downloadable datasets observed include complexity rankings and growth projections; country trade by product at HS92 1-, 2-, 4-, and 6-digit levels; total trade by country; and total trade by product. The displayed years were 1995–2024, with files reflecting Comtrade data last retrieved on 15 Dec 2025. The page states that users can query data through a GraphQL API, with access documentation on GitHub.

The page explains that Atlas bilateral trade data reconciles exporter and importer reports with reliability weighting and harmonizes product classifications over time. Input sources named are UN Comtrade for goods, IMF DOTS for services, IMF WEO for economic indicators, and FRED’s Producer Price Index for Industrial Commodities for price adjustments. It distinguishes HS classifications for contemporary products from SITC for long-run analysis, states that annual updates typically occur after country reporting lags, and requests appropriate citation of Atlas and underlying sources. It also warns that bulk downloads and visualizations can differ slightly because of refresh and revision timing.

## OECD Inter-Country Input-Output tables
URL: https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/inter-country-input-output-tables.html

The OECD describes ICIO as a statistical infrastructure mapping production, consumption, and investment within countries and international trade in goods and services between countries, broken down by country and economic activity. The page describes regular ICIO covering 80 economies plus the rest of the world and extended ICIO covering 80 countries with China and Mexico split to account for firm heterogeneity; the extended form is used for OECD global-value-chain indicators.

The page states that the current database was released in mid-January 2026 as a second revision of a 2025 database and that there were no changes to geographic coverage, economic activities, or years covered. Related OECD datasets include greenhouse-gas footprints, gross-output flows in global value chains, input-output tables, multinational enterprises and global value chains, trade in employment, and a pass-through-frequency indicator measuring repeated exposure to suppliers from a particular country.

## Evidence discipline

These observations directly verify access and methodological descriptions on the official pages. They do not independently validate every number or model output generated by the datasets. For Forge World, Atlas is strongest for product-country capability proxies and trade-network structure; OECD ICIO is strongest for country-industry dependencies and value-added/embodied-flow analysis. Neither directly measures plant-level operating capability, tacit know-how, or current production readiness.
